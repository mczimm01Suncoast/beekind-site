How it works

One kind act. Two taps.
A little more honey.

    Do something kind

    Say a kind word. Help someone. Say thank you. Kindness to yourself counts too.

  2. Said a kind word

    Pick what you did. Two taps, and it is in your jar. You can add your own kind acts to the list.

Always near

Your bee goes with you

    A jar that fills, on your Home Screen.

  • Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

    Your jar shows after your first kind act of the day.

  • Apple Watch

    Log a kind act from your wrist.

  • iPhone and iPad

    Both show the same jar, with iCloud.

  • Gentle reminders

    A kind idea each day, and a nudge in the evening if your jar is not full. Only if you turn them on.

Private by design

Your kind acts are saved on your devices and in your own iCloud. We cannot read them. Bee Kind has no account, no ads and no tracking. The app sends anonymous usage data to help us make it better. You can turn that off in Settings.
