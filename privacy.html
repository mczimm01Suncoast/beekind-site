Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Bee Kind is made by MZ Technology Solutions LLC.

What the app sends to us

To make Bee Kind better, the app sends us two kinds of data. Neither has your name, and neither is linked to you.

  • Usage counts: which parts of the app people use. For example: a kind act was logged, the kind of act, and a jar was full.
  • Crash and error reports: what went wrong, with the model of your device, its version of iOS and the version of the app.

The app never sends the name of your bee, the name of a kind act that you make, or other text that you type. We do not store your IP address. Bee Kind has no account, no ads and no tracking. We do not sell this data, and no one uses it for ads.

Two companies process this data for us in the United States: PostHog (usage counts) and Sentry (crash and error reports).

To turn it off, open Bee Kind, then Settings, and turn off "Share usage data".

Your kind acts: on your devices and in your own iCloud

Your kind acts, your daily goal and your list of kind acts are saved on your iPhone or iPad, and on your Apple Watch if you use it. If iCloud is on, a copy is saved in your own iCloud, so that each of your devices shows the same jar. Only you can read it. We cannot read it, and we get no copy.

Your bee's name and look and your other settings are saved on your device only.

A picture leaves your device only when you share it with the share button.

Payments

Apple handles all payments. We do not see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have a plan.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, your iPhone makes and shows it. It does not use a server.

Children

Bee Kind is for a general audience. The usage data has no name, and it is not linked to a person of any age.

How to delete your data

To delete the kind acts on a device, delete the app from it. If iCloud is on, a copy stays in your iCloud. To delete that copy too: open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap iCloud. Tap Storage. Tap Bee Kind, then tap "Delete Data from iCloud".

The usage data has no name, so we cannot find the data of one person. To stop it, turn off "Share usage data" in Bee Kind, under Settings.

This website

This website has no cookies, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The company that hosts the website can keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service safe.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

Questions

Write to support@beekind.io. If you write to us, we use your email address and your message only to answer you.
