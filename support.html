Bee Kind: Spread Kindness

Questions or ideas? Write to us.

support@beekind.io

On an Android phone? Read Bee Kind on Android.

Change or cancel your plan

Open Bee Kind, then Settings, then "Manage plan". You can also open the Settings app of your iPhone, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.

Restore your plan

Open Bee Kind, then Settings, then "Restore purchases". This also brings back a Lifetime plan.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. You can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A new iPhone, or a deleted app

Bee Kind has no account. Your kind acts are saved on your iPhone. If iCloud is on, a copy is in your own iCloud. On a new iPhone with the same Apple Account, open Bee Kind, and your kind acts come back. If you delete the app, your kind acts leave that device. With iCloud on, they come back when you install Bee Kind again.

Your plan stays with your Apple Account. To get it back, install Bee Kind and tap "Restore purchases".

iPhone and iPad

With iCloud on, Bee Kind shows the same jar on each device that has your Apple Account. To check it, open Bee Kind, then Settings: "iCloud sync" says "On". We cannot read your kind acts.

Apple Watch

Bee Kind for Apple Watch comes with the iPhone app. It shows your jar, and you can add a kind act there. It needs Bee Kind on your iPhone.

Reminders

You turn the daily reminder and the evening nudge on or off in Bee Kind, under Settings. You choose the times there too. Reminders also need your OK for notifications in the Settings app of your iPhone.

Bee Kind on Android

To change or cancel your plan, open Bee Kind, then Settings, then "Manage plan". You can also open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, then tap Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

To restore your plan, open Bee Kind, then Settings, then "Restore purchases". Use the Google Account that bought the plan.

Google Play handles all payments and refunds. Read about refunds in Google Play Help.

Your kind acts are saved on your phone. If backup is on in Android, a copy is in your Google Account backup, and it comes back on a new phone or when you install Bee Kind again. If you delete the app, your kind acts leave the phone.
