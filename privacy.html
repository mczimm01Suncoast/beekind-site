Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Bee Kind is made by MZ Technology Solutions LLC.

We collect no data

We collect no data. Bee Kind has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your data.

Your data stays on your devices

Your kind acts, your daily goal, your bee's name and look, and your settings are saved on your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch if you use it. They do not leave your devices, except a picture that you choose to share with the share button.

Payments

Apple handles all payments. We do not see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have a plan.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, your iPhone makes and shows it. It does not use a server.

Children

Bee Kind is not made for children under 13.

How to delete your data

To delete your data, delete the app from your iPhone and your Apple Watch.

This website

This website has no cookies, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The company that hosts the website can keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service safe.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

Questions

Write to support@beekind.io. If you write to us, we use your email address and your message only to answer you.
