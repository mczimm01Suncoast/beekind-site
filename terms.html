The license

On iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, your use of Bee Kind is under Apple's standard license agreement for apps: the Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Plans

Bee Kind is free to download. After a short story and your first kind act, choose a plan to keep going:

  • Yearly: $19.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial
  • Monthly: $3.99 a month
  • Lifetime: $39.99, one payment

Payment and renewal

On iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or at the end of the free trial. A subscription renews automatically unless you turn it off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in the settings of your Apple Account. Prices are in US dollars.

On Android

On Android, you get Bee Kind from Google Play, and the Google Play Terms of Service apply. Payment is charged to your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase, or at the end of the free trial. A subscription renews automatically until you cancel it. You can manage or cancel it in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions. Google Play shows the price in your currency.
